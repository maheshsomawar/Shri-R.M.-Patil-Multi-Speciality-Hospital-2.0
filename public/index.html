Patient Privacy Notice

Shri R.M. Patil Multi Speciality Hospital & Trauma Care collects only the minimum details needed to confirm your OPD appointment, process online consultation payment, and send confirmation SMS.

  • Your mobile number is never displayed publicly or exposed in URLs.
  • Checking an appointment from home requires both your Appointment Number and registered 10-digit mobile number.
  • Medical notes, prescriptions, and laboratory reports are never returned through public status checks.
